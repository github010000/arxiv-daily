## [5/10] On the stability of learning

- **저자**: John J. Vastola
- **공개일**: 2026-10-27
- **링크**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35856v1

**요약**

Sparse and noisy gradients in biological neural networks can cause asymptotically infinite variance, rendering learning unstable despite unbiased credit assignment. The study derives explicit stability conditions for single- and multi-layer linear networks. This establishes that sufficiently frequent and informative feedback is a necessary constraint for reliable learning beyond mere unbiasedness.
