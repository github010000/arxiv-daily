## [5/10] BrainWideBench: Benchmarking large-scale pretraining and across-animal transfer in multi-region neural recordings

- **저자**: Alexandre Andre, Shivashriganesh P. Mahato, Vinam Arora
- **공개일**: 2026-09-21
- **링크**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22064v1

**요약**

Multi-region neural recordings across many animals and brain regions enable learning general-purpose neural representations that can be transferred across diverse downstream tasks. The BrainWideBench benchmark evaluates these representations on three complementary task suites: decoding behavior, predicting masked or future neural activity, and recovering biologically meaningful anatomical organization. The benchmark systematically evaluates pretraining methods across transfer settings, including finetuning on downstream objectives and zero-shot generalization to unseen animals.
