## [6/10] On the Complexity of the Compatibility Problem for Succinctly Encoded Conditional Distributions

- **저자**: Guy Emerson
- **공개일**: 2026-08-31
- **링크**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31120v1

**요약**

Succinctly encoded conditional distributions represented as **arithmetic circuits** are analyzed to study tractability trade-offs in probabilistic modeling. The **Compatibility Problem** is proven to be **co-NP-complete** or **PSPACE-complete** depending on zero-probability constraints, marking it as computationally intractable compared to the table-based case. This establishes fundamental limits for high-dimensional neural network modeling and demonstrates that compatible conditionals may lack succinct joint representations.
