## [5/10] What Breaks Under Pruning in Smart Homes, and When? Evaluating LLM Degradation Across Architectures and Task Complexity

- **저자**: Congjing Zhang, Vashishtha Patil, Henning Lange
- **공개일**: 2026-09-16
- **링크**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17515v1

**요약**

Pruning-induced LLM degradation in smart-home tool calling was evaluated across various architectures and task complexities. MoE models demonstrate superior resilience to pruning compared to dense models, which suffer from sharp accuracy drops. These findings suggest that assessing action-level specificity, rather than just aggregate accuracy, is crucial for reliable tool execution.
