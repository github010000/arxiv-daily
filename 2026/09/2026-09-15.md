## [1/10] Type Diversity Enables Transformers to Generalise Compositionally

- **저자**: Anssi Moisio, Mathias Creutz, Mikko Kurimo
- **공개일**: 2026-09-14
- **링크**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13144v1

**요약**

Lexical and structural **type diversity** equally influence **compositional generalisation**, refuting claims that structural tasks are inherently harder for **Transformers**. By creating linguistically diverse variants of the **COGS** and **SLOG** datasets via **Grammatical Framework**, we demonstrate that **type diversity** is the critical factor for generalisation. This challenges the prevailing notion of **compound divergence** and highlights the need to consider dataset properties in evaluating **Transformer** capabilities.
