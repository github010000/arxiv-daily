## [8/10] Across-Design Uncertainty in Short Pricing Panels: Evidence from Simulated Price Trajectories

- **저자**: Pedro Cadahia Delgado
- **공개일**: 2026-08-21
- **링크**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21334v1

**요약**

short pricing panels의 across-design 불확실성을 분석하기 위해 gradient-boosted 모델과 Paule-Mandel 분산 성분 추정법을 활용하였다.
